The GST Lifecycle

Before · During · After

The mistakes that cost businesses the most happen at the boundaries. Here is what a compliant operator does at every stage — and where most firms quietly slip.

Stage 01

Before Registration

  • Choosing the right business structure
  • PAN, Aadhaar, address & activity proofs
  • HSN / SAC classification strategy
  • Composition vs regular scheme analysis
  • State-wise place-of-supply mapping
Stage 02

During Registration

  • GST portal application & ARN generation
  • Officer verification & query handling
  • Avoiding the common rejection reasons
  • Authorised signatory & DSC mapping
  • GSTIN issuance & certificate vault
Stage 03

After Registration

  • Tax-invoice system & numbering policy
  • Monthly GSTR-1, GSTR-3B & IFF
  • ITC reconciliation with 2A / 2B
  • E-Way Bills, E-Invoicing thresholds
  • Annual return, audit readiness, notices
